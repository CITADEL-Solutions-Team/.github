## Who we are

CITADEL Solutions, LLC is an Illinois-based technology company. We build websites and provide IT and computer services for small businesses, homeowners and individuals.

We're a team of cybersecurity students and developers, so security isn't an add-on: it's built into everything we make, from how a site is coded to how it's hosted and maintained.

## What we do

- **Web development:** custom sites and ready-to-launch templates, built to be fast, accessible and secure.
- **Hosting and maintenance:** we keep sites updated, monitored and secure after launch.
- **Computer and IT services:** setup, repair and support for home and small-business machines.

## How we work

**We practice what we sell.** Before we protect your systems, we built and secured our own.

- **Security first.** Secure defaults, least-privilege access, reviewed code and secret scanning in CI.
- **Accessible by design.** We build to WCAG 2.1 AA.
- **Privacy-respecting.** Cookieless analytics, no ad trackers, and hardware security compliance. We only take necessary data to get the job done.
- **Built on recognized standards.** We follow CIS Controls and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework in how we run our own systems.
